# hold — Philosophy

Comprehension at the human's pace. A move takes something that already exists and hands back what a human works through at their own pace, until they can say how it works with the agent out of the room.

---

The human is the world model. What lands in a head is the product; the artifact is the vehicle, and a vehicle nobody worked through taught nothing.

Pace is the difference. What a reader works through at their own speed stays; what arrives on the turn clock is gone by the next pull.

What is held decides the move, never who. A change, a behavior, and one thing to keep each take a different vehicle; who reads it only sets the brief.

A register that biases every turn is a stance, not a move. A voice that must be re-triggered decays; a stance is set once and persists.
