# Missed move

A moo move that would have improved the flow, had it fired. Nobody named it.

**Sign:** a stretch of turns whose trouble one installed moo skill exists to prevent, and no `[skill]` line for it. Take each skill's trigger from its `description` in the installed list `read.sh` prints.

| Counts | Doesn't |
|---|---|
| The trouble is on the page: a steering turn, a redone change, an answer the user had to supply twice | A skill that would only have added a step to a flow that went well |
| The skill's trigger, as its description states it, matches the turns | A match you reach only by stretching the trigger's words |

**Evidence:** the turns that show the trouble, the skill, and the words of its description that match them.
