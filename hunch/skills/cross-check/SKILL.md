---
name: cross-check
description: Have the agents behind rival answers to one question check each other's answers, then pick on the evidence. Use when independent answers to one question disagree, or would, and no test, file or run can settle which is right, or when one attempt at an artifact would lock in its shape.
---

Before any answer exists, write 3 to 6 criteria a right answer meets, each one you can grade. Authors never see them.

The authors answer blind. No answers yet → ask two or three subagents, each on a different model, the same question with the same files; an artifact goes in its own folder per author.

Write down the pick you would make from the answers alone.

Send each author the other answers, never its own, by message to the same agent. Ask:

- per rival: what it gets right that yours misses, and what breaks if it is wrong, each with a file:line, quote or number
- whether the rivals change your pick, and why

A critique with no evidence goes back to its author once.

Pick on evidence, criterion by criterion, never on how many authors back an option. Then walk each losing answer once for a part the pick lacks and a criterion favours, and fold it into the pick by hand, so the result stays one design.

Hand back:

- the pick, in one sentence
- per option: the fact that decides it, where it lives, who raised it
- the strongest objection to the pick, who raised it, and whether it held
- each graft, and the answer it came from
- what the critique and the grafts changed from the pick you wrote down
- what stays open

The answers agree, or one check settles the question → settle it yourself.

The answers diverge wildly, sharing no shape → the question was loose: reframe it and ask again, never pick between them or average them.
