# Words

A word steers a default the model already has. It is said inline, lasts one utterance, and needs no skill — the bare model complies when asked plainly. Composers cite words by saying the line, never by loading anything.

| Word | The default it steers | The line to say |
|---|---|---|
| **compress** | complete, restated, verbose output | "Give me the minimal prompt/statement that covers this." |
| **ROUTER** | the agent does the work itself, in its own context | "You are a ROUTER, not a worker. You spawn agents and verify their output." |

Evidence a word suffices: the two mined compress turns (`.scratch/moves/all-turns.jsonl` ids 38, 57) are plain one-line asks — "give me the minimal prompt" — and the model complied with no skill loaded. ROUTER is the one line that survived delegate's retirement.
