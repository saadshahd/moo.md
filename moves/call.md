# Call

A **call** is a decision the agent makes on its own, handed back at the highest level the human can react to.

Nothing else catches a wrong call, so surface it as a call and keep it cheap to reverse. Never where reversing would cost more than asking.

|           | Needs                                       | Gives                                              | You're in the wrong one if                   |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Judge** | something that exists, and a claim about it | a verdict, cited where the human can check it fast | there is nothing to evaluate yet → **Draft** |
| **Draft** | a choice the human cannot make yet          | something built, with the choices it made surfaced | they could have specified it → **Clarify**   |

The standard behind a call is the agent's own. That is what makes it useful in a domain the human is ignorant of, and it is why the reaction surface is the bound.
